Valued by what actually sells

Values come from recent eBay sold listings in your region, not asking prices, with outliers screened out. They follow the quality you pick, from Near Mint down to Damaged, or a PSA, BGS or CGC grade.

  • 12 currencies using European Central Bank reference rates
  • Portfolio view with value over time, cost basis and profit
  • Price alerts and a cross-region price comparison

Set up in five minutes

Pullfolio runs in your browser and installs to your home screen like an app. There's nothing to download from an app store.

  1. Open Pullfolio

    Go to pullfolio.pages.dev on your phone or computer.

  2. Sign in with email

    Enter your email and tap the link we send. No password to remember. Use the newest link if you request more than one.

  3. Add to your home screen

    iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

  4. Add your cards

    Import a spreadsheet from Settings, or tap + on any card in the Catalog.

  5. Make it yours

    In Settings choose your region, currency, light or dark mode, and whether eBay links open in the app or your browser.

To update after a new version is released, close Pullfolio fully and open it again.

Where the data comes from

Pullfolio brings together open card databases and official sources, and credits each one.

Card catalogs

TCGdex, the Pokémon TCG API and Scryfall, plus community card databases for One Piece, Dragon Ball and Yu-Gi-Oh.

Sold prices

eBay's official APIs, filtered to your region. Pullfolio is not affiliated with eBay.

Exchange rates

European Central Bank reference rates via Frankfurter, checked hourly.

Card images and logos

Belong to their publishers and are shown only to identify cards and sets. See Credits.
