Pullfolio

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

Pullfolio is a trading card collection tracker run by Giulio Giannetti in Victoria, Australia. This policy explains what Pullfolio stores about you, why, who handles it, and how to have it removed. Questions or requests: pullfolio.app@gmail.com.

What we store

InformationWhy
Your email addressTo send sign-in links. There are no passwords.
Your collection, wishlist, carts, prices paid, storage locations and notesThis is the app's purpose.
Condition photos you uploadTo show the state of a card you list or trade.
Friends, trade proposals, messages and ratingsTo run trading between accepted friends.
Your vendor page handle and listingsShown publicly only if you switch the vendor page on.
Preferences such as region, currency, theme and filtersKept on your own device (browser storage), not on our servers.

Pullfolio does not use advertising, does not sell or rent your information, and does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Who sees it

  • You. Your collection is private by default.
  • Accepted friends see your wishlist and the cards you mark as tradeable.
  • Anyone with your link can see a shared binder or a vendor page you have switched on.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the database, sign-in and uploaded photos, hosted in Sydney, Australia.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • eBay: Pullfolio requests sold-listing data from eBay's official APIs. Your personal information is not sent to eBay. When you follow an eBay link, eBay's own privacy notice applies.
  • Image and font hosts: card images, logos and fonts load from their publishers' and providers' servers, which can see your device's IP address, as with any website.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Delete items at any time in the app. Ask us to delete your account and everything stored about you will be removed within 30 days, apart from anything we must keep by law.

Your choices

  • Export your collection at any time from Settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, a correction, or deletion: pullfolio.app@gmail.com.
  • If you're unhappy with how we handled a request, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change and significant updates will be shown in the app.
